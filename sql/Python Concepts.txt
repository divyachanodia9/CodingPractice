## Time Complexity: Rate of increase in time with respect to input size.
Rules of Time Complexity -
1) Always calculate TC in terms of wrost case.
2) Avoid constant values.   TC: O(8N2 + 3N2 + 15) -- Avoid 15
3) Avoid Lower bounds     

Different types of TC:
1) Big-Oh O(n) - wrost case
2) Theta - Average case
3) Omega - Best case
